Their story

Bounty hunter from Psamathe, raised in the Clessidra Rossa clan and trained in archery from a young age as its expected future leader. His restless streak clashed with the clan's strict code, and in 2736 one reckless choice got him cast out of the order and his family, forcing him to leave his younger sister Aurora behind. He worked alone, with his cat Artemis, until a bystander recruited him for the Apex Games.
