Their story

Racer from Salvo whose family drove in Redline, a violent motorcycle battle royale; her father Klaus died competing in it. Over her mother's objections she joined and became its top racer, with Weaver as her only rival. Apex Games commissioner Kuben Blisk offered the Apex Card to the season's winner, and after a dangerous move on the final lap she woke in a hospital to be handed the card and told that Weaver had died.
