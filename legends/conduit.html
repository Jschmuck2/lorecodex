Their story

Born on Nexus, where a rebellion against the monarchy ended when a Monarch Titan's pilot detonated its battery, leaving an irradiated quarantine zone. A fan of the Apex Games from the start in 2729, she retrieved that battery to power an exosuit built with help from her community, and fought in local rings to support her family while the radiation slowly harmed her. She became the first Nexus resident invited to the Games, over the objections of her sister Diwa, and later wondered whether the other Legends were truly friends or just polite.
